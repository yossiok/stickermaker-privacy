Privacy Policy

Sticker Maker from AI (package com.lutraapps.stickermaker), published on Google Play by LutraApps. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Who we are

This app is published by LutraApps, operated by Yossi Oknin in Kiryat Gat, Israel. This page describes how the Android app handles data. For questions, email the Google Play developer account at yossibimbatoknin@gmail.com.

Images and sticker packs stay on your phone

The app slices sticker sheets, adds captions and compresses stickers on your device. Images you pick with the Android Photo Picker, and the packs you save, are not uploaded to LutraApps servers. The app does not create an account and does not ask for broad storage permission.

Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent (UMP)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. In regions where consent is required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before requesting personalized ads. You can change or withdraw that choice later from the app's privacy options, where Google requires them, and you can also reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • Device or other IDs, including the advertising ID, used for advertising or marketing.
  • App interactions (how the app is used in connection with ads), used for advertising or marketing.

This data is sent over an encrypted connection. Collection of these advertising identifiers is optional in the sense that you can decline personalized ads through the consent form or device settings. Ads may still be shown in a non-personalized form where allowed. If you buy Remove ads, the app stops requesting and showing ads.

One-time purchase (Google Play Billing)

You can remove ads with a one-time, non-consumable in-app product called Remove Ads, sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. LutraApps does not receive your payment card number or full payment details.

The app collects purchase history for the Remove Ads product so it can unlock the ad-free experience and restore a previous purchase on the same Google Play account. This data is required for that feature. It is not shared with advertising partners for advertising. Payment and entitlement records are also held by Google under Google Play's policies.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, precise or approximate location (it does not request location permission), photos for our servers, or browsing history. We do not sell your data.

Data retention and deletion

Sticker images and packs stored by the app are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall it. LutraApps does not keep a copy of those files. Advertising and purchase records held by Google are retained under Google's policies. The app does not offer a separate in-app form to request deletion of data held by Google; you can contact us at yossibimbatoknin@gmail.com and we will help where we can, and you can use Google account and Play tools for ads and purchases.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
